Æther — Theremin DAW

Mouse Mode

  • Click & Drag on the surface to play
  • X-axis → Pitch (left = low, right = high)
  • Y-axis → Volume (top = quiet, bottom = loud)

Keyboard Mode

  • A–; row for white keys
  • W E T Y U O P for sharps/flats
  • Z / X to shift octave down / up

Gesture Mode (Webcam)

  • Right hand up / down — pitch (higher = higher note)
  • Wobble the right hand for natural vibrato
  • Pinch thumb–index (right hand) for ±1 semitone fine tuning
  • Left hand height — volume (raise = loud, rest low = silent)
  • Close a left fist to mute; hide the right hand to stop playing
  • Calibrate button (top-right) matches the pitch and volume fields to your reach

Controls

  • R — Toggle recording
  • Space — Stop playback / recording
  • Tab — Cycle mouse / keyboard / gesture mode
  • ? — Toggle this help

MIDI Playback

  • Open the Tracks panel to import a .mid file
  • MIDI notes play through the theremin with current effects
